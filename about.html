Our Story

Three divisions, one standard

Groenhouten Dairy Farm — Biologische Zuivelboerderij Groenhouten — is an organic, specialised agricultural operation in Leuth, Netherlands, focused on livestock and on-farm retail.

Our 30-hectare property in the Ooijpolder region produces raw milk and raw-milk cheeses, and raises the dairy cattle behind every bottle and wheel. Bulk and export buyers get priority access, and early reservations are recommended during high-demand periods.

We believe in transparency from pasture to table. Every order — retail or bulk — is handled with care by a team dedicated to making sure what leaves the farm is exactly what you asked for.

📍 Located at 6578 JB Leuth, Netherlands, near the German border — serving households, cheesemakers and corporate buyers across the Netherlands and the wider EU.
